WhatsApp Auto-Wish Bot.

An event-driven bot that wishes people in group chats only when a wish is really happening, and spends almost nothing on AI calls to know it.

When2026
StackNode.jsBaileysGeminiRailway
LinksCode ↗

Architecture

WhatsApp pushes every group message to the bot over a WebSocket. Most messages are dropped by cheap local checks before anything costs money; only a real cluster of wishes reaches Gemini.

Every drop happens before the next, more expensive stage, so a busy group costs the same as a quiet one until people actually start wishing someone.
